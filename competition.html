Rules

  1. Your own game. You made it, or you made it with a team you're part of. Free assets and tutorials are fine, but no reuploads of someone else's game.
  2. Made any time. It doesn't have to be new. Finished games, demos and works in progress all count.
  3. Accepted links: a Roblox game, a GitHub link (a repo or a GitHub Pages game) or an itch.io page. Want to send another kind of link? Check with me on Discord first.
  4. It has to be playable. The link must be public and work when I open it on stream. Downloads need to run on Windows. I play each game for about 10 minutes, so make sure the fun part shows up quickly.
  5. One entry per person. Pick your best one. Need to fix your entry? Edit your post in #competition-submissions.
  6. Keep it stream friendly. Nothing hateful, sexual or gross. I can skip any game I can't show on stream.
  7. Your entry counts once it's posted in #competition-submissions on my Discord, before the deadline: .
  8. The Discord vote picks the winner. The game with the most votes when the poll closes on Tuesday, December 8 wins. If there's a tie, I break it. Voting for your own game is fine, but no alt accounts or vote trading. I can remove entries or votes that break the rules, and I'll post any rule change here.

Questions

Can I enter a game I made a long time ago?

Yes. Any game you've worked on counts, no matter when you made it.

Can I enter a game I made with friends?

Yes, as long as you were part of the team. Pick one person to enter it, and that person gets the prize if it wins.

My game is on Game Jolt, Newgrounds or somewhere else. Can I still enter?

Probably. Message me on Discord with the link first so I can make sure I can play it on stream.

How do I get my prize if I win?

I'll message you on Discord after the stream, and we'll sort out the easiest way to get your Robux or gift card to you. If you asked for a different gift card, I'll try to get that one, but if I can't, you can pick Robux or Amazon instead.

How is the winner picked?

After I play every entry on stream, I put up a poll in my Discord. Everyone in the server can vote until Tuesday, December 8, and I reveal the winner on stream that day. If there's a tie, I pick the winner from the tied games. Join the Discord to vote.

Will you play every game?

Yes. I'll spend about 10 minutes testing every entry that follows the rules on Saturday's stream. Put your best stuff near the start and add tips in "How to play" so I see the good parts in that time.

Watch it live, then vote

Every entry gets about 10 minutes on stream on Saturday. Subscribe so you don't miss your game coming up, and join the Discord to vote for the winner.
